Hi, I'm Tom. I'm a solo developer in Australia, and Black Tallow is the game I'm making.

## What is Black Tallow?

It's an old-school co-op dungeon crawler with directional combat. You roll a character the way you would at
the table, with stats, race, class, background and burdens. Then you and your party hexcrawl across a grim
fantasy world, load up the cart, and head down into the dark. You can play alone or with up to two friends.
Other parties are down there too, and what happens when you meet them is up to you.

Characters die for good. The cart, and whatever you've stashed in it, rolls on.

## What this devlog is for

I'll post here as the game comes together: what I'm working on, what went wrong, and why things work the way
they do. Some posts will get fairly technical, but I'll explain the jargon as I go, so you don't need to make
games to follow along.

Two posts go up alongside this one, about what I'm working on right now:

- [Tuning the swing](/devlog/tuning-the-swing/): making sword fights feel heavy and easy to read.
- [Getting characters into the game](/devlog/characters-into-the-game/): the new race bodies, faces and
  clothes, and the small fixes that take the longest.

## Where it's at

Black Tallow is in development, and plenty will change along the way. That's part of what this devlog is for:
showing the game as it is, not as it will be.
