This is the template post. It shows everything a post can hold, so you can see how each piece looks.
Write in plain Markdown: a blank line starts a new paragraph.

## A heading

Use `##` for a section and `###` for a part inside one. The post's title is the page's only top heading,
so a post starts at `##`.

### A smaller heading

A list:

- one thing
- another thing
- a third, with **bold** and *italic* words and [a link](/devlog/)

A numbered list:

1. first
2. second

## Pictures

Put picture files in `src/devlog/img/`. PNG or JPEG is fine: the build turns every picture into WebP at the
right sizes. A plain picture:

![A lit candle on a dark floor.](img/example-candle.png)

A picture with a caption under it:

{% figure "img/example-candle.png", "A lit candle on a dark floor.", "A caption sits under the picture, in smaller grey type." %}

The text in quotes after the file name is the alt text: say what the picture shows, for people who cannot
see it. Every picture needs one.

## Quotes and code

> A quotation sits indented, with a line down its left side.

Code goes between lines of three backticks:

```
{% raw %}{% figure "img/your-picture.png", "What the picture shows.", "An optional caption." %}{% endraw %}
```

And `short code` sits inside a sentence.
